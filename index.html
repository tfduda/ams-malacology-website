Our Mission & Programs

The AMS is a nonprofit scientific organization focused on the study and conservation of mollusks with several hundred active members. Our primary activities include hosting annual meetings, awarding student research grants, and publishing the American Malacological Bulletin.

History

Founded by Norman W. Lermond as the American Malacological Union, the society was established to connect professionals and amateur naturalists, holding its first meeting in Philadelphia in 1931. The name was changed to the American Malacological Society in 1998 to reflect its focus and improve organizational functions.
